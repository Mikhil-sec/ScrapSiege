Scrap Siege

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026 · Applies to the Android application com.mikhilnaika.scrapsiege

Scrap Siege is an augmented-reality tabletop strategy game developed and published by Mikhil Naika ("we", "us"), an individual developer. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and what your choices are. It covers the Scrap Siege Android app distributed through Google Play, and nothing else.

The short version

  • There are no accounts and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email address or date of birth.
  • The camera is used only to place the game board on a real surface. Camera images are processed on your device in real time and are never recorded, stored, or transmitted anywhere.
  • There is no advertising, no advertising identifier, and no analytics or tracking SDK in the app.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your own device and is deleted when you uninstall.
  • The one thing that does leave your device is subscription data, if you choose to buy Scrap Siege Pro. It goes to Google Play and to our purchase-management provider, RevenueCat, so that your subscription works.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

1. Camera, motion sensors, and augmented reality

Scrap Siege is an AR game: it uses your device's rear camera and motion sensors to find a flat surface — a table or a floor — and to keep the virtual battlefield locked to it as you move around. This is done on your device by Google Play Services for AR (ARCore).

  • Camera frames and motion data are used live, in memory, for tracking only.
  • The app does not save photos or video, does not write camera data to storage, and does not upload camera or sensor data to us or to anyone else.
  • The app does not perform face recognition, object recognition, or any machine-learning analysis of what the camera sees. There is no AI or ML model in the app at all.
  • The app does not use ARCore Cloud Anchors or any other feature that would send scene data off the device.

You are asked for camera permission the first time you start a match, and Android lets you revoke it at any time in Settings → Apps → Scrap Siege → Permissions. Without camera permission the AR gameplay cannot run, but no other part of the app is affected.

ARCore is a Google component and its own data handling is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and the ARCore data disclosure.

2. Purchases and subscriptions

Scrap Siege offers one optional paid subscription, Scrap Siege Pro. If you never buy it, nothing in this section applies to you.

Payment is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see, receive or store your card number, billing address, or any other payment credential — Google processes the payment and tells us only that a valid purchase exists.

To keep your subscription working across reinstalls and devices, we use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA) as a data processor. When you make or restore a purchase, the following is sent to RevenueCat's servers:

DataWhy
A randomly generated anonymous app user ID To attach a subscription to an installation. It is generated by the SDK, is not derived from your identity, and is not linked to your Google account, your email, or any advertising ID.
Purchase receipt / transaction data from Google Play To verify with Google that the purchase is genuine, to acknowledge it (Google refunds unacknowledged purchases), and to determine whether your subscription is currently active.
Device model, operating system version, app version, SDK version Sent automatically with each request; used to process the receipt correctly and to diagnose purchase failures.
IP address, and the country inferred from it Inherent to making a network request; used by RevenueCat for currency/region handling and abuse prevention. We do not use it to locate you and the app never requests location permission.

We do not set any custom attributes, we do not send your email address or name (the app does not have them), and we do not link purchase data to any advertising profile. What we see is limited to anonymous, aggregated subscription information: how many subscriptions are active, in which country, and whether purchases are succeeding.

RevenueCat's handling of this data is governed by the RevenueCat Privacy Policy. Google Play Billing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Managing or cancelling your subscription

Subscriptions renew through your Google Play account and are cancelled there: open the Google Play Store app → your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Scrap Siege. We cannot cancel or refund a subscription on your behalf; refunds are handled under Google Play's refund policy.

3. Data stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of data locally, using Android's standard app-private storage. It never leaves your device and is not backed up to us:

  • Your best star rating for each level.
  • Whether you have muted the sound.
  • A local flag recording whether your Pro subscription was active at last check, so the game does not have to wait for the network on every launch.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. There is nothing to export because none of it identifies you.

4. What we do not collect

Stated explicitly, because "we may collect" language in most policies hides this. Scrap Siege does not collect, request, or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or date of birth.
  • Location data. The app has no location permission of any kind.
  • Contacts, photos, media, files, SMS, call logs, calendar, or microphone audio.
  • Your Android Advertising ID, or any other advertising or cross-app tracking identifier.
  • Analytics or telemetry. There is no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, and no ad network integrated into the app.
  • Camera images, video, audio, or 3D scans of your surroundings.
  • Health, financial, or biometric data.

We do not sell personal information, we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we have no advertising partners.

6. Children

Scrap Siege is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app has no accounts, no chat, no user-generated content, and no advertising. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

7. Legal bases for processing (UK/EU users)

  • Performance of a contract — processing purchase and subscription data so that the subscription you bought works, and so it can be restored.
  • Legitimate interests — verifying receipts to prevent fraudulent purchases, and diagnosing purchase failures.

Camera and sensor data are processed transiently on your device and are not transmitted, so no transfer occurs.

8. International transfers

RevenueCat, Inc. and Google LLC are based in the United States, so subscription-related data described in section 2 is processed there. Both provide contractual safeguards for international transfers under their respective privacy policies and data processing terms.

9. Retention

  • On your device: until you uninstall or clear the app's data.
  • Purchase records at RevenueCat: retained for as long as needed to manage the subscription and to meet tax and accounting obligations, then deleted.
  • At Google: governed by Google's own retention practices for Play transactions.

10. Your rights, and how to delete your data

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal information, to request a portable copy, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

Because the app has no accounts, the practical path is:

  • Local data: uninstall the app, or use Settings → Apps → Scrap Siege → Storage → Clear data. This removes everything stored on the device immediately, without contacting us.
  • Purchase records: email us at the address below and we will delete the associated customer record held by RevenueCat. Please note that deleting it also removes our ability to restore an active subscription to a new device, and that Google Play keeps its own record of the transaction independently of us.

We will respond to a request within 30 days.

11. Security

All network traffic from the app uses HTTPS. The app holds no secret keys capable of granting paid access — entitlements are validated server-side by RevenueCat against a Google-signed receipt. The app stores no personal data on the device that could be exposed if the device were lost.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the "last updated" date at the top will change and the updated version will be published at this same URL. Because the app has no accounts, we have no way to notify you directly — please check back here if you want to stay current.
